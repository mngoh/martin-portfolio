    Assault Victims in Los Angeles

    Black women in LA are assaulted at 3 to 6 times the rate of Hispanic and White women, and nothing we measured explains why. 174,827 LAPD assault reports, 2020 to 2023, as rates against census population.

    How I built it Every assault geocoded to its census tract, denominators from ACS age bands, six rival explanations tested, then a Poisson adjustment ladder with ACS and LAHSA covariates.

    Python pandas statsmodels Chart.js
